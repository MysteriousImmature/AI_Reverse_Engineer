01 / METHOD

A disciplined inference loop

Reverse engineering here means building a testable behavioral model—not reconstructing proprietary code, data, or parameters.

01QUESTION

Frame a hypothesis

Convert curiosity into a falsifiable claim about one observable behavior.

02CONTROL

Design a controlled probe

Vary one factor at a time while fixing model, prompt shape, and parameters.

03EVIDENCE

Collect repeated trials

Capture outputs, token counts, timing, errors, refusals, and run metadata.

04ANALYSIS

Compare signatures

Look for stable patterns, boundaries, discontinuities, and competing causes.

05RESULT

Report with confidence

Separate observations from inference and document uncertainty explicitly.

02 / EXPERIMENTS

Probe one dimension at a time

Choose a probe family. Each card gives you a hypothesis, controlled method, evidence signal, caveat, and copyable starter script.

    03 / EVIDENCE

    Inference is not extraction

    A reliable assessment ties every conclusion to an observable signal and states how strongly the data supports it.

    ✓
    DEFENSIBLE

    What you can estimate

    • Effective input boundaryUsing accepted size, recall, and error behavior.
    • Relative randomnessUsing repeated trials under fixed parameters.
    • Tokenizer tendenciesWhen documented token usage is available.
    • Instruction behaviorAcross harmless roles, formats, and history.
    • Service reliabilityLatency, errors, tool calls, and consistency.
    ×
    NOT RELIABLY RECOVERABLE

    What remains unknowable

    • Exact model weightsOutputs do not uniquely determine billions of parameters.
    • Private training corpusSimilar text is not proof of membership or provenance.
    • Hidden instructionsBehavioral clues do not establish exact prompt text.
    • Internal architectureSeveral implementations can create the same signature.
    • Model versus wrapperRouting, retrieval, policy, and templates can look like model traits.
